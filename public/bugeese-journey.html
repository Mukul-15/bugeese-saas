The beginning

An idea, before a restaurant

Bugeese began with a dream: to give India its own dedicated vegetarian burger brand.

June 2023 · Jabalpur

Founded in Jabalpur

A chef trained in five-star hotels set out to show that vegetarian food can be bold, flavourful and satisfying, with handcrafted burgers at the centre.

January 2024 · Second outlet
